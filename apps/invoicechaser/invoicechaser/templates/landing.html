How it works

  1. Add your invoices. Customer, amount and due date. That's all we need.
  2. See what's late. Every invoice is labelled Due soon, Overdue or Paid, based on today's date.
  3. Send a follow-up. Pick the situation, from a friendly heads-up to a final polite reminder, then copy it or open it in your email app.
  4. Mark it paid. Your dashboard updates instantly so you always know what's outstanding.

Messages you can trust

Every amount, date, name and invoice number in a follow-up comes straight from your records. Messages never add discounts, fees, legal threats or payment details you didn't enter, and nothing is sent without you.
