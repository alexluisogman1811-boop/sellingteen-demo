Products and suppliers

SKU, name, category, supplier, cost, selling price, quantity and a reorder threshold for each product. Search by name or SKU and filter by category, supplier or stock status.

Stock in, stock out, adjustments

Record each movement with a date and reason. Stock can't go below zero by mistake: corrections are made with an adjustment that needs a reason.

Low-stock alerts

Low and out-of-stock items are marked clearly, counted on the dashboard, and collected in a printable reorder list grouped by supplier.

Full stock history

Every change is kept with the quantity before and after, and can't be edited or deleted, so you can always see what happened.

CSV import and export

Bring in your product list from a spreadsheet, with a preview that checks every row first. Export products and history any time.

Your own private account

Each shop signs up for its own account and only ever sees its own products, suppliers and history.

Good to know

  • StockPing does not connect to Shopify, Amazon or accounting software.
  • You add stock by hand or by importing a CSV file.
