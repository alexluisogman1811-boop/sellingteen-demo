Privacy Policy

Last updated: {{ updated }}

What we collect

  • Your account: your name or studio name, email address, and a secure hash of your password (never the password itself).
  • What you enter: your clients and their contact details, websites, tasks, the results and notes you record, evidence files you upload, issues and saved reports.
  • Basic technical logs (time, page, IP address) to keep the service secure and working.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your data or your clients' data.
  • We don't contact your clients.
  • We don't use advertising or tracking cookies. We use one cookie to keep you logged in.
  • We never see your card details. Payments are handled by our payment provider, which has its own privacy policy.

Why we use it

Only to run the service for you: to show your records, make your reports, keep your account secure, manage your subscription, and answer your questions.{% if mail_on() %} If you ask for a password reset, we email you a reset link. We never send marketing email.{% endif %}

Where it is stored

On the server that hosts SiteCare Desk, protected by your login. Each account can only see its own data. A report you share is visible to anyone who has its private link, until you turn the link off.

Your clients' information

You add your clients' details, so you are responsible for having a good reason to keep them and for following privacy laws where you operate. Delete a client at any time and its websites, records and saved reports are removed.

Your choices

You can export all your data as CSV files, and delete your whole account and all its data yourself, on the Account page. It is removed straight away.
