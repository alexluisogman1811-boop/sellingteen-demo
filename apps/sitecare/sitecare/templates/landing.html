What you can do

  • Keep your clients and their websites (domain, platform, maintenance plan, status) in one place.
  • Set recurring tasks: daily, weekly, monthly or every N days.
  • Record each check with the date, the result, who did it, notes and optional evidence files.
  • Track issues with severity, status and resolution notes.
  • Make a client report for a month or any date range. Print it, download it as HTML, or export CSV.
  • Save a report as an unchangeable copy and share it with that client through a private link.

Honest by design

  • Everything is manual. A task is only marked done when you record it.
  • SiteCare Desk does not run backups, uptime monitoring or security scans, and never assumes they succeeded.
  • Missed work stays overdue until you record or skip it. It never silently disappears.
  • Each studio has its own account and only ever sees its own data. Your clients never need an account.
