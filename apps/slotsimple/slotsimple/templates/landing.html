Your booking page

  • Your services with prices and durations
  • A calendar that only shows days with free times
  • Customers give their name, email and optional notes

  • Customers can look up or cancel with their reference and email

Your rules

  • Opening hours and weekly availability
  • Blocked dates and times (holidays, breaks)
  • Buffer time between bookings and minimum notice
  • How far ahead customers can book
  • No double bookings, checked again on the server when each booking is saved
  • All times shown in your business's timezone

Your dashboard

  • Confirm, cancel, reschedule and complete appointments
  • Add phone or walk-in appointments yourself
  • Upcoming, completed and cancelled views, with search
  • Printable daily schedule and CSV export

Good to know before you start

  • One calendar per business: SlotSimple suits one practitioner, room or resource. It doesn't schedule several staff members separately.
  • SlotSimple does not send emails, SMS messages or calendar invites. Customers see their confirmation on screen, and you contact them yourself when something changes.
  • Customers can cancel online; rescheduling is done by you.
  • No online payments or deposits for appointments.
