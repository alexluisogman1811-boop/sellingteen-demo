Privacy Policy

Last updated: {{ updated }}

What we collect

  • Your account: business name, email address and a secure hash of your password (never the password itself).
  • What you enter about your customers: name, optional company, email and phone, and their invoices (number, amount, issue and due dates, notes, status, and sent and paid dates).
  • Follow-up history: which reminders you marked as sent or skipped, the date, and the message text.
  • Basic technical logs (time, page, IP address) to keep the service secure and working. Search terms are left out of logs.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your data or your customers' data.
  • We don't send messages to your customers, and we don't collect payments from them.
  • We don't use advertising or tracking cookies. We use one cookie to keep you logged in.
  • We never see your card details. Payments for your subscription are handled by our payment provider, which has its own privacy policy.

Why we use it

Only to run the service for you: to show who owes you money, recommend follow-ups, draft reminders, keep your account secure, manage your subscription, and answer your questions.

AI reminder drafts

When AI drafting is turned on, writing a reminder sends only the facts needed for that draft to our AI provider (Anthropic): the customer's name, invoice number, amount, due date, days overdue, reminder stage and your business name. Customer emails, phone numbers and invoice notes are not sent.

Where it is stored

On the server that hosts PayRecover, protected by your login. Each business can only see its own data.

Your customers' information

You add your customers' details, so you are responsible for having a good reason to keep them (for example, you invoiced them) and for following privacy laws where you operate (such as Thailand's PDPA or similar laws). Delete a customer at any time and their details and invoices are removed.

Your choices

You can change or delete your customers and invoices at any time. You can delete your whole account and its data yourself on the Account page; it is removed straight away. You can also {% if support_email %}email {{ support_email }} from your account email{% else %}contact us{% endif %} and we will do it within 30 days.
