How it works

  1. Add the invoices you've sent. “Due soon” and “Overdue” are worked out from the due date.
  2. Each day, the dashboard shows who to follow up with.
  3. Review the drafted reminder, copy it, send it yourself, then mark it as sent.
  4. When a customer pays, mark the invoice as paid. Follow-ups stop and the amount is added to your recovered revenue.

Reminders, in order

Days overdueReminder
1Friendly reminder
4Second reminder
8Stronger reminder
14Final reminder

Reminders are at least 3 days apart. Drafts and paid invoices never get follow-ups.

What it is not

  • It never sends messages itself. It drafts a reminder, and you copy it into your own text or email.
  • It is deliberately not a CRM or an accounting system.
