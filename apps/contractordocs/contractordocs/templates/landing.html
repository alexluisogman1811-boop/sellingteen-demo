What it does

  • Keeps each subcontractor's documents in one place: upload PDFs or photos (PNG, JPEG, WEBP).
  • Records each document's type, upload date and expiration date.
  • Tries to read the type and expiration date from the file. You check and confirm them.
  • Never guesses a date: if none is clearly printed, the document is marked Needs review.
  • Dashboard of Valid, Expiring soon (30 days or less), Expired and Missing documents, based on the document types you require.
  • In-app reminders 30, 14 and 7 days before a document expires, and again once it has expired.
  • Search and filter by subcontractor, document type and status.

What it doesn't do

  • It tracks the documents and dates you upload or confirm. It does not verify that a document is genuine.
  • It does not give legal, insurance or compliance advice, or tell you whether you or a subcontractor are compliant.
  • Reminders appear in the app only. No emails are sent.
