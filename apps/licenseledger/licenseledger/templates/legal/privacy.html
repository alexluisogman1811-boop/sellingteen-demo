Privacy Policy

Last updated: {{ updated }}

What we collect

  • Your account: business name, email address and a secure hash of your password (never the password itself).
  • What you enter about your business: products, customers (name, email, company, notes), orders (reference, date, amount, statuses, payment reference you type), licenses, renewals, delivery notes and reminder templates.
  • License keys are never stored in readable form. We keep only a one-way hash of each key and its last 4 characters.
  • No card numbers. The app refuses text that looks like a payment card number.
  • Basic technical logs (time, page, IP address) to keep the service secure and working. Anything that looks like a license key is removed from logs.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your data or your customers' data.
  • We don't send messages to your customers.
  • We don't use advertising or tracking cookies. We use one cookie to keep you logged in.
  • We never see your card details. Subscription payments are handled by our payment provider, which has its own privacy policy.

Why we use it

Only to run the service for you: to show your records, keep your account secure, manage your subscription, and answer your questions. If email is switched on, we use your email address only to send password reset links you ask for.

Where it is stored

On the server that hosts LicenseLedger, protected by your login. Each business can only see its own data. When you use "Demo data & reset", a backup copy of your account's data is saved on the server first.

Your customers' information

You add your customers' details, so you are responsible for having a good reason to keep them (for example, they bought from you) and for following the privacy laws where you operate. You can edit or delete a customer at any time.

Your choices

You can download all your data at any time (Settings, "Export all my data"). You can also delete your whole account and all its data yourself (Settings, "Delete my account"), including your reset backups. It is removed straight away.
