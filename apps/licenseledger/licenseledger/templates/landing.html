LLLicenseLedger

Keep track of the licenses you sell

LicenseLedger is a simple tracker for small digital-product sellers. Record your orders, customers, license keys and renewals by hand, in one dashboard.

  • Orders with separate order, payment and delivery status (payment status is what you record; the app never checks payments).
  • License types: lifetime, fixed term, or managed by hand. See what expires soon and what has expired.
  • Secure license codes. Keys are stored only as a hash and shown in full just once, when they are issued.
  • Renewal history, revocation and key replacement, with a full history for every license.
  • Renewal reminder templates you copy with one click. Nothing is sent automatically.
  • CSV import (with a dry run) and CSV export.

LicenseLedger is not a payment processor or a license-validation server. It does not connect to any store and does not send email to your customers.
