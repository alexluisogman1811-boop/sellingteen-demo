Never let a roof estimate go cold.

A simple follow-up list for small roofing contractors who track leads in a notebook, a spreadsheet or their head, and don't want a full CRM.

Join the free pilot Log in

1. Add your leads

Type them in or import your spreadsheet. Name, phone, job, estimate value, and when to call back.

2. Open today's list

Every morning, see who's overdue, who's due today and who has no next step. Tap a number to call.

3. Follow up and log it

One tap logs the call and books the next follow-up. Get a suggested text you can edit and send yourself.

  • Free during the pilot. No card is needed and there's nothing to cancel.
  • Your data stays yours. Export it or delete it any time. Each business only sees its own leads.
  • LeadRescue never messages your customers. It reminds you, and you decide what to send.
  • We're working with a small number of roofing companies and will ask for 15 minutes of feedback each week.
