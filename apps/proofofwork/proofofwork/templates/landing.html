ProofOfWork · For cleaning, repair and maintenance businesses

Record completed work. Share a clean report with your customer.

Your team checks off each task on their phone, adds before-and-after photos and notes, and records the reason for anything that couldn't be done. ProofOfWork turns that into a printable completion report you can share by a link you can revoke.

For your workers on site

  • Each task is marked Done, Not completed (a reason is required) or Not checked. There is no "mark all done" button.
  • Before and after photos, up to 8 at a time, with an area label so they're paired on the report. Location data is removed from photos.
  • Large phone photos are shrunk on the phone before uploading.
  • Report notes for the customer, and internal notes the customer never sees.

For managers

  • Customers, properties and reusable checklist templates.
  • A job can't be completed until every required task is done or has a reason, and any exception must be explicitly confirmed, which is recorded with the person's name.
  • A history of who recorded, submitted, approved or shared each job.
  • Search, filters and CSV export.

Reports your customers can trust to be what you recorded

  • No AI. Nothing in a report is generated: every line comes from data your team saved and photos they uploaded.
  • Appointment times are shown as scheduled; work times come from actions recorded in the app. Approval is labelled as approval by your team, not by the customer.
  • Customer links show the report only: never internal notes, property access notes or job history. Revoke a link at any time.
  • A report is a record of what your team entered. It is not a signature, a certification or independent verification of the work.
