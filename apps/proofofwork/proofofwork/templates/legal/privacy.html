Privacy Policy

Last updated: {{ updated }}

What we collect

  • Your business account: business name, and for each team member their name, email address and a secure hash of their password (never the password itself).
  • What your team records: customers and their contact details, property addresses and access notes, jobs, checklist results, notes, and before-and-after photos.
  • Photos: stored re-encoded, with location (GPS) and camera details removed when uploaded.
  • Basic technical logs (time, page, IP address) to keep the service secure and working. Customer report link codes are removed from logs.

Who can see it

  • Only the people in your business account. Each business can only see its own data.
  • Your customers can see a job's completion report only through a link you create. It never shows internal notes, property access notes or job history. You can revoke a link at any time.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your data or your customers' data.
  • We don't use AI or send your data to AI services.
  • We don't use advertising or tracking cookies. We use one cookie to keep you logged in.
  • We never see your card details. Payments are handled by our payment provider, which has its own privacy policy.

Why we use it

Only to run the service for you: to store and show your jobs and reports, keep your account secure, manage your subscription, and answer your questions.

Your customers' information

You add your customers' details and photos of their properties, so you are responsible for having a good reason to keep them (for example, they hired you) and for following privacy laws where you operate (such as Thailand's PDPA or similar laws). You can delete customers, jobs and photos at any time.

Your choices

You can change or delete your records at any time. A manager can download all of the business's data (CSV files and photos) and delete the business account with all of its data from the Account page. Deleting is immediate and cannot be undone. If you need help{% if support_email %}, email {{ support_email }} from your account email{% else %}, contact us{% endif %}.
