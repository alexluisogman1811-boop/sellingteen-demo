Getting started

How do I create my first quote?

After signing up, the setup steps ask for your business name, services and prices. Then go to New quote, paste a customer's message exactly as you received it, and click Generate Quote. Review the price and reply, edit anything you like, then click Copy Reply and paste it into your email or messaging app.

Does QuoteFlow send replies to my customers?

No. QuoteFlow never contacts your customers. You always review the reply and send it yourself.

Can I change my business details later?

Yes — everything is in Profile. Changes apply to new quotes; quotes you've already generated keep their original details.

Prices and quotes

How is the price calculated?

From your pricing rules only. For example, with a deep clean starting price of $200 that includes 2 bedrooms and 1 bathroom, an extra bedroom at $30 and an extra bathroom at $25, a 3-bed, 2-bath deep clean is $200 + $30 + $25 = $255. Add-ons the customer asks for are added on top. Each quote shows this breakdown under the price.

Why does my quote say "From $…"?

The customer didn't give enough detail (usually the number of bedrooms or bathrooms), so QuoteFlow shows your starting price and the reply asks them for the missing details.

What does "Missing information" mean?

Something needed for an accurate quote isn't in your profile — for example, a price for an add-on the customer asked for. QuoteFlow lists it instead of guessing. Add it in your Profile, and check the reply before sending.

How do I add add-on services?

In Profile → Pricing rules → Add-on services, put one per line as Name: price, for example Inside oven: 45.

Why is there no time estimate?

Time estimates come from the durations in your services list. Add them like "Deep clean – about 4–5 hours for a 2-bed home".

A customer asked for something I don't offer. What happens?

If it isn't in your services list, the reply politely says it's not something you offer, and it's flagged for you to check.

Can I change the price or reply?

Yes. Edit the price, duration, included services and reply, then click Save Quote. You can mark a quote as Sent to keep track.

The AI made a mistake. What should I do?

Edit the reply before sending — you're always in control. If it keeps happening, email us the example so we can improve it.

Plans and billing

What's included in each plan?

Free: {{ plans.free.quota }} generated quotes per month. Pro: ${{ plans.pro.price }}/month for {{ plans.pro.quota }} generated quotes per month. Everything else is the same.

When do my quotes reset?

On the 1st of each month (UTC). Quotes that fail to generate don't count towards your limit.

How do I upgrade, update my card, or cancel?

Go to Account → Plan & billing. Payments are handled securely by Stripe — we never see or store your card details. If you cancel, Pro stays active until the end of the period you've paid for.

My payment failed. Will I lose access?

Not straight away. Stripe retries the payment automatically, and you keep Pro while it does. Update your card from Plan & billing to fix it.

Privacy

What happens to the customer messages I paste in?

They're sent to our AI provider to generate the quote and saved with the quote in your account. Only you can see your quotes. See our Privacy Policy for details.
