Quoting eats into your evenings

Every inquiry needs a price worked out and a careful reply written. When you're busy cleaning, replies wait — and customers book whoever answers first.

Inquiries arrive all day

Texts, emails and website forms come in while you're on a job, then pile up for the evening.

Every quote is a little different

Extra bedrooms, an oven, a deep clean instead of a regular one — working out the right price takes time.

Slow replies lose jobs

A customer who waits a day for a price has often booked someone else.

Rushed replies look unprofessional

Short, inconsistent answers make it harder to win customers who are comparing cleaners.

Set up your prices once. After that, every quote takes seconds — and you always check it before it's sent.
