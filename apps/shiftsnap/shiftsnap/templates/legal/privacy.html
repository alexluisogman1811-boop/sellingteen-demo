Privacy Policy

Last updated: {{ updated }}

What we collect

  • Your business account: business name, email address, and a secure hash of each password (never the password itself).
  • What managers enter about employees: name, role, optional email and phone, notes, availability, shifts, and whether they have a sign-in.
  • Sign-ins: usernames, the last sign-in time, and failed sign-in attempts (kept for a day, to block password guessing).
  • Basic technical logs (time, page, IP address) to keep the service secure and working.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your data or your employees' data.
  • We don't send schedules, emails, SMS or notifications to your employees or anyone else. The only email ShiftSnap can send is a password reset link, and only to the person who asked for it (when this option is switched on).
  • We don't use advertising or tracking cookies. We use one cookie to keep you signed in.
  • We never see your card details. Payments are handled by our payment provider, which has its own privacy policy.

Who can see what

Each business can only see its own data. Inside a business, managers see everything; employees with a sign-in see their own shifts and the team schedule, but not other employees' contact details, availability or notes.

Why we use it

Only to run the service for you: to show and check your schedule, keep accounts secure, manage your subscription, and answer your questions.

Where it is stored

On the server that hosts ShiftSnap, protected by sign-in.

Your employees' information

You add your employees' details, so you are responsible for having a good reason to keep them and for following privacy laws where you operate (such as Thailand's PDPA or similar laws). You can edit or remove an employee at any time; removing them deletes their details and sign-in.

Your choices

Managers can change or remove employees and shifts at any time, or clear all of the business's employees and shifts on the "Demo & data" page. A manager can download all of the business's data ("Export all my data") and delete the whole account with all of its data ("Delete my account") on the Account page at any time. You can also {% if support_email %}email {{ support_email }} from your account email{% else %}contact us{% endif %} and we will delete it within 30 days.
