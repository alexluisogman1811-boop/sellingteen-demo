What it does

  • Day and week schedule with filters by employee and role, plus unfilled shifts you can assign later.
  • Overlapping shifts are blocked: the same person can't be booked twice at once, including across midnight.
  • Availability warnings: a shift outside the hours someone said they can work needs a deliberate “Save anyway”.
  • Weekly hours for every employee, with unfilled hours and totals; 40h+ is highlighted.
  • Overnight shifts (for example 22:00 to 06:00) are handled correctly.
  • Print the week or export a CSV to share it however you like.
  • Employee sign-ins: your staff can see their own shifts and the team schedule, read-only. They don't see each other's contact details or private notes.
  • Works on phones: a day picker, a quick add button and large buttons for small screens.

What it doesn't do

ShiftSnap does not send schedules, emails, SMS or notifications to anyone. You share the schedule yourself: print it, export the CSV, or give your employees a sign-in to check it.
