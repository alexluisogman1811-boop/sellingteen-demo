How it works

  1. 1

    Make a checklist

    Start from a reusable template for that type of project, or add items by hand. Each item is a question, a file request or an action, with a due date.

  2. 2

    Send one private link

    Each client gets a private portal link that expires. Your clients never need an account or password.

  3. 3

    Your client fills it in

    They answer questions, upload the files you asked for and mark actions done — on their phone or computer.

  4. 4

    You review

    Approve each submission, or ask for a correction with a note the client sees in their portal.

What you get

  • A dashboard of active onboardings, overdue items, what's waiting on clients and what's waiting for your review.
  • Progress for every client, split into the client's items and your own.
  • Safe file uploads. File type, content and size are checked, and files are never opened in the browser.
  • Private notes. Internal comments and notes are never shown to your client.
  • Search, filters and CSV export of your onboardings and tasks.

You stay in control

  • ClientStart sends no emails. Reminder messages are written for you to copy and send yourself, from your own email.
  • Each client sees only their own items — never other clients, and never your private notes.
  • Turn a link off any time. Extend it, disable it, or regenerate it so the old link stops working.
  • Your data stays yours. Each business account only ever sees its own clients and files.
