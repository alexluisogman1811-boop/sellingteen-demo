Privacy Policy

Last updated: {{ updated }}

What we collect

  • Your account: business name, email address, and a secure hash of your password (never the password itself).
  • What you enter about your clients: contact details, project details, private notes, checklists and comments.
  • What your clients submit through their portal: answers, messages and the files they upload.
  • Basic technical logs (time, page, IP address) to keep the service secure and working.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your data or your clients' data.
  • We don't send emails or messages to your clients, and we never send you marketing email. If email is set up, the only email we send is a password reset link you ask for.
  • We don't use advertising or tracking cookies. We use one cookie to keep you logged in and protect forms.
  • We never see your card details. Payments are handled by our payment provider, which has its own privacy policy.

Why we use it

Only to run the service for you: to show your clients' onboarding, let your clients use their portal, keep your account secure, manage your subscription, and answer your questions.

Where it is stored

On the server that hosts ClientStart. Each business can only see its own data. Each client portal shows only that client's own items, and private notes are never shown to clients. Uploaded files are stored under random names and only downloaded through your account or that client's own portal link.

Your clients' information

You add your clients and invite them to upload information, so you are responsible for having a good reason to collect it and for following privacy laws where you operate (such as Thailand's PDPA or similar laws). Delete a client at any time and their details and files are removed.

Your choices

You can change or delete your clients and their files at any time. You can download all your data as CSV files under Account (Export all my data). You can delete your whole account yourself under Account (Delete my account): this removes all its records and uploaded files straight away.{% if support_email %} You can also email {{ support_email }} from your account email and we will do it within 30 days.{% endif %}
