The problem

"What did I pay for?"

Customers weren't there when the work happened. A bare invoice doesn't show them what was done.

Photos lost in your camera roll

Before and after shots are scattered across your phone and hard to send in one place.

Who still owes you?

Tracking unpaid jobs in your head, on paper or in texts makes it easy to lose track.

How it works

  1. Create the job. Customer, address, price. Under two minutes.
  2. Add photos and notes. Before and after photos, and what you did.
  3. Send the report. Your customer gets a clean page with the work, photos and amount due.
  4. Track payment. Mark it paid when the money arrives. Your dashboard shows what's outstanding.

Features

Professional job reports

A mobile-friendly page for each job with your business name, the work done and the total.

Before & after photos

Upload from your phone. Location data is stripped automatically.

Unpaid work at a glance

See completed jobs that haven't been paid, and the total outstanding.

Optional AI job summary

Turn rough notes into a clear summary. It only rewords what you wrote, and you approve it first.

Private share links

Each report has its own unguessable link. Your other jobs and customers stay private.

Online payments: coming later

For now, customers see your payment instructions and you mark jobs paid yourself.
