Planning only — ContentQueue never posts for you. It doesn't connect to Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, LinkedIn, X or YouTube and never asks for your social media passwords. “Scheduled” means planned inside ContentQueue; you publish on each platform yourself, then mark the post as published.

Content calendar

Month and week views. Click a day to plan a post, or drag a post to another day. On phones the calendar becomes an agenda list, and there's a printable version.

A clear workflow

Idea → Draft → In review → Scheduled (planned) → Published (confirmed by you) → Archived. Overdue posts are flagged so nothing slips through.

Captions ready to copy

Write captions with a live character count for each platform's limit, add hashtags, and copy the caption, the hashtags or both with one click.

Templates and an idea bank

Reusable caption templates with fill-in-the-blank fields, and an idea bank you can turn into posts later. No AI service involved.

Campaigns and categories

Group posts by campaign and content category, then search and filter by platform, status, campaign, category or date.

Your content stays yours

Each business only ever sees its own posts, captions and images. Export everything to CSV at any time.
