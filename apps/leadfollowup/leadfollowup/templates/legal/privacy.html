Privacy Policy

Last updated: {{ updated }}

What we collect

  • Your account: business name, email address, and a secure hash of your password (never the password itself).
  • What you enter about your leads: name, phone, email, where the lead came from, the service they're interested in, notes, status, follow-up dates, and the contact history you record.
  • Basic technical logs (time, page, IP address) to keep the service secure and working. Search terms are removed from logs.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your data or your leads' data.
  • We don't send messages to your leads.
  • We don't use advertising or tracking cookies. We use one cookie to keep you logged in.
  • We never see your card details. Payments are handled by our payment provider, which has its own privacy policy.

Why we use it

Only to run the service for you: to show your leads and follow-ups, write follow-up messages, keep your account secure, manage your subscription, and answer your questions.

Message writing

Follow-up messages are normally written from built-in templates on our server. If AI writing is switched on, then when you ask for a message only what is needed to write it (the lead's name, service, status, source, notes and most recent recorded contact, plus your business name) is sent to our AI provider.

Where it is stored

On the server that hosts LeadFollowUp AI, protected by your login. Each business can only see its own leads.

Your leads' information

You add your leads' details, so you are responsible for having a good reason to keep them (for example, they asked you about a service) and for following privacy laws where you operate (such as Thailand's PDPA or similar laws). Delete a lead at any time and its details and contact history are removed.

Your choices

You can change or delete your leads at any time. On your Account page you can download a copy of all your data (CSV files) and delete your whole account and all its data yourself.{% if support_email %} You can also email {{ support_email }} from your account email for help.{% endif %}
