Privacy Policy

Last updated: {{ updated }}

What we collect

  • Your account: business name, email address, a secure hash of your password (never the password itself), and your website if you add one.
  • What your clients submit through your links: their name, optional business or role, their feedback, an optional rating, an optional website, and whether they gave permission to publish.
  • What you enter: project names and private notes, and notes about permission you recorded yourself.
  • Basic technical logs (time, page, IP address) to keep the service secure and working. Share links are shortened in logs.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your data or your clients' data.
  • We don't send messages to your clients.
  • We don't use advertising or tracking cookies. We use one cookie to keep you logged in; your public pages and submission forms set no cookies.
  • We never see your card details. Payments are handled by our payment provider, which has its own privacy policy.

What is public

Only testimonials you approved and whose author gave permission are shown on your public pages, and only the client's name, business, rating and feedback. Pending, rejected and archived submissions, clients' websites, and all notes stay private.

Why we use it

Only to run the service for you: to collect and show your testimonials, keep your account secure, manage your subscription, and answer your questions.

Where it is stored

On the server that hosts TestimonialTap, protected by your login. Each account can only see its own data.

Your clients' information

You decide whom to ask for feedback and what to publish, so you are responsible for following privacy laws where you operate (such as Thailand's PDPA, the GDPR, or similar laws), including honouring a client's request to withdraw or delete their testimonial. You can withdraw permission or delete a testimonial at any time and it disappears from your public pages straight away.

Your choices

You can change or delete your projects and testimonials at any time, and download all your data as CSV files from your Profile page. You can delete your whole account and all its data yourself, at any time, with "Delete my account" on your Profile page. It is removed straight away.
