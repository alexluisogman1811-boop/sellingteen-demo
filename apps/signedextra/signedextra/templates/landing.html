Get extra work signed on site, or put on written record when the GC won't sign.

SignedExtra is a phone-friendly app for small electrical subcontractors. Record extra work with photos, get the superintendent's signature, prepare a written notice when they won't sign, and turn signed work into a change-order invoice.

Start your {{ trial_days }}-day free trial Log in

1. Record it on site

The foreman notes what changed, who asked for it, hours by worker type, materials and photos. Foremen don't see prices.

2. Get it signed

The superintendent signs on the phone, or through a one-time link (no account needed), where they can also decline with a reason. Signed tickets lock with a content fingerprint and a history that can't be edited.

3. Notice or invoice

If the GC refuses or doesn't respond, prepare a dated written notice that you send yourself. The office prices signed or noticed work and prints a change-order invoice.

Built for small shops

  • Owner, Office and Foreman logins for the whole company
  • Dashboard of work waiting on a signature, unpaid or disputed
  • Export all your company's data any time

Good to know

  • SignedExtra doesn't send email or text messages. You share links and send notices and invoices yourself.
  • It doesn't collect payments from your customers and doesn't connect to QuickBooks or Procore.
  • Written notices are records you send yourself, not legal advice, and they don't guarantee payment. Check your contract and local rules.
