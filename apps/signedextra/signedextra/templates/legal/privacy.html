Privacy Policy

Last updated: {{ updated }}

What we store

  • Company and team accounts: company name and contact details, each user's name, email, role, and a secure hash of their password (never the password itself).
  • What your team enters: GCs and their contacts, jobs, labor rates, extra-work tickets, prices, written notices and invoices.
  • Photos and signatures: uploaded photos, and the name, title, optional contact details and drawn signature of the person who signs or declines a ticket, with the time, IP address and browser of that signature. Photos and signed tickets are stored with a content fingerprint (SHA-256) so changes can be detected.
  • A history log of actions in your account (who did what, when, from which IP address).
  • Basic technical records to keep the service secure, such as recent failed logins and request counts per IP address for rate limits.

What we don't do

  • We don't send email or text messages to you, your team or your GCs.
  • We don't sell your data.
  • We don't use advertising or tracking cookies. We use one cookie to keep you logged in (and to protect forms).
  • We never see card details. Subscription payments are handled by our payment provider, which has its own privacy policy.

Who can see it

Only your company's users, according to their role (foremen only see their assigned jobs and, by default, no prices). A person you send a signing link to can see that one ticket's work and photos until the link is used, replaced or expires.

Where it is stored

On the server that hosts SignedExtra. Each company can only see its own data.

Your choices

  • The Owner can export all company data (records, photos and signatures) from the Account page.
  • The Owner can permanently delete the company from the Account page. Team logins, tickets, photos, signatures, notices, invoices and links are deleted straight away.
  • History log: it is append-only by design, so its entries are kept after a company is deleted. They are no longer linked to an account and record actions, times, names of the people involved (including the name a GC typed when signing), IP addresses and some details such as GC contact names. They don't contain ticket descriptions, photos, signature images, prices or notice text.
